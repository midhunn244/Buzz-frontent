Our story

Right on the sand at Papanasam Beach

Buzz & Breeze is a beachfront cafe and restaurant in Varkala, serving diverse dishes and drinks in a relaxing atmosphere. Come for lunch, stay for the sunset, and enjoy friendly hospitality with the sea right in front of you.

  • 🌊 Sea views
  • 🌅 Sunsets
  • 🌴 Outdoor seating
  • 🎵 Live music
  • 📶 Wi-Fi
  • 🤝 Friendly hospitality
